Fully Funded Scholarships Without IELTS: The Duolingo Route 2027

How to Use the Duolingo English Test to Skip IELTS and Still Win Full Funding.

1. Why the Duolingo English Test Matters for Scholarship Hunters

For most scholarship applicants, the language test is the first real obstacle — not the essay, not the recommendation letters. IELTS and TOEFL can cost between $200 and $340 per attempt, require an in-person test center, and take up to two weeks to deliver results. The Duolingo English Test (DET) was built to remove exactly that friction.

It is taken entirely online, from home, in under an hour, costs roughly $65–$69, and delivers a certified score within about 48 hours. It is now recognized by thousands of universities and a growing number of scholarship programs worldwide, which makes it one of the most practical shortcuts for students who are short on time, budget, or access to a physical testing center.

2. How the Test Works

Format

A single adaptive test combining reading, writing, speaking, and listening tasks, taken on your own computer with webcam proctoring — no test center required.

Delivery

Results arrive within roughly 48 hours, and you can send your score report to as many universities or programs as you like at no extra cost.

Because it is cheaper and faster than traditional tests, many students now take it first, then decide whether a specific program still requires IELTS or TOEFL on top of it.

3. Score Guide: Duolingo vs. IELTS

Scholarship pages rarely list Duolingo requirements directly — they list an IELTS-equivalent. Use this as a general reference, then confirm the exact number on the program's official page.

4. Where the Duolingo English Test Is Actually Useful

1
University-Specific Scholarships

Over a thousand universities worldwide, including many in the US, UK, and Canada, now accept DET directly for both admission and merit-based scholarship applications. Always check the specific department, since acceptance can vary even within the same university.

2
Government and Foundation Programs

Some national scholarship schemes accept DET as one of several approved English proficiency tests. Others still require IELTS or TOEFL specifically — this is the single detail most applicants overlook, so verify it before you register for any test.

3
Duolingo's Own Fee Waivers

Through its Access Program, Duolingo offers free test vouchers to eligible students from underserved or low-income backgrounds — worth checking before paying out of pocket.

5. Expert Tips for Success

  • Confirm before you pay: Search "[scholarship name] + Duolingo" on the program's official site before registering for the test — acceptance changes yearly.
  • Take it early: Since results arrive in 48 hours, there's little reason to wait until the last week of a deadline — leave room for a retake if your score falls short.
  • Send it everywhere: Score reports are free to send to unlimited institutions, so submit to every program you're considering, even backups.
  • Pair it with strong documents: A DET score alone rarely wins a scholarship — committees still weigh your research plan, GPA, and recommendation letters heavily.
